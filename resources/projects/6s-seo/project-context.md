# 6s SEO Tool - Marketer Workflow & Ranking Workspace
## Project Reference Context

**Project Label:** 6s SEO Tool  
**Role:** Product Designer  
**Scope:** Keyword tracking, ranking inspection, opportunity prioritization, and client reporting  
**Format:** SaaS product design for digital marketing agency workflow  
**Status:** Product design exploration / prototype

---

## 1. Problem Framing & Core Tension
SEO tools suffer from severe metric-overload and report-hopping.
- **The Core Problem:** Marketers drown in tabular CSV data without knowing where to look first, how to isolate consequential rank shifts, or how to explain ranking movements to clients.
- **The Design Goal:** Move from raw metrics to actionable decisions. "The useful unit is not another metric. It is a decision the marketer can explain."
- **Information Architecture:** Establish a 3-tier cognitive journey:
  1. *Overview:* Fast signal detection across domains/clients.
  2. *Investigation:* Deep keyword/ranking context with active filter preservation.
  3. *Client Communication:* Transforming raw analysis into a clean, stakeholder-ready briefing.

---

## 2. Key Decisions & Tradeoffs
1. **Overview as an Entry Point, Not a Destination:**
   - Designed summary cards that immediately point to high-impact changes rather than dumping endless table rows.
2. **Context Preservation Across Filters:**
   - Filters refine the dataset without clearing active context or burying the user's starting intent.
3. **Actionable Opportunity Framing:**
   - Highlighting rank positions 4–10 (striking distance keywords) where modest optimization yields maximum traffic gains.
4. **Client-Facing Translation:**
   - Providing structured summary modules that translate algorithmic shifts into clear business language for non-technical stakeholders.

## 4. Truth & Guardrails
- **Guardrail:** Do not claim that manual CSV reporting was eliminated in production, or claim verified client time savings without real operational data.
- **Truth Rule:** Only use "Opportunity Matrix" if it is named in the verified design artifacts; otherwise describe it functionally as striking-distance keyword prioritization.
